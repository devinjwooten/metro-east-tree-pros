# Local SEO Strategy

## Objective

Earn qualified organic traffic for tree-service needs in the Metro East by
publishing useful, accurate service content that answers homeowner questions
and leads naturally to an estimate request or phone call.

## Search intent and page roles

| Intent | Primary route | Customer need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General tree services | `/` and `/services` | Understand offerings and request help |
| Tree removal | `/services/tree-removal` | Resolve a hazardous, dead, or unwanted tree |
| Tree trimming | `/services/tree-trimming` | Improve health, clearance, appearance, and safety |
| Stump grinding | `/services/stump-grinding` | Reclaim usable property after removal |
| Emergency service | `/services/emergency-tree-service` | Address an urgent tree hazard safely |
| Storm cleanup | `/services/storm-cleanup` | Recover from wind or storm damage |

Each route needs one unique search intent, one descriptive H1, supporting H2s,
relevant FAQs, a clear CTA, and links to related services. Do not create thin
city pages or duplicate service pages solely to target phrase variants.

## Local coverage

Use “Metro East” as the broad geographic descriptor. Mention Belleville,
O'Fallon, Edwardsville, Collinsville, Fairview Heights, Swansea, Shiloh,
Columbia, and Waterloo only where it helps users understand coverage. A
scannable service-area section is preferred over repeating city names in body
copy.

All locations, service claims, addresses, hours, and phone numbers must be
verified with the business before publication. Do not invent city-specific
projects, customer stories, or response-time promises.

## On-page standards

- Lead with the service outcome and problem solved, not keyword repetition.
- Use semantic headings and descriptive internal links.
- Add useful details: warning signs, what to expect, safety guidance, cleanup,
  timing, and estimate process.
- Keep title and description metadata unique and consistent with visible copy.
- Use descriptive image alt text only when an image conveys information.
- Include a relevant estimate CTA near the hero and at the end of the page.

## Authority and structured data

When verified business information is available, implement accurate JSON-LD for
`ProfessionalService` or `LocalBusiness`, then add `Service`, `FAQPage`, or
`BreadcrumbList` only where visible content supports it. Never mark up sample
reviews, unverified ratings, or unsupported claims.

## Measurement

Before adding analytics tooling, define event names and consent requirements.
At minimum, measure estimate-form submissions, click-to-call actions, primary
CTA clicks, and organic landing-page performance. Review search queries and
conversion paths regularly; prioritize content gaps that represent real
customer needs.
