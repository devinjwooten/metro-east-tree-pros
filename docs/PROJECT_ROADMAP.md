# Product Roadmap

This roadmap is a prioritization guide, not a promise. Validate business facts,
budget, legal requirements, and technical dependencies before scheduling work.

## Phase 1 — Production foundations

1. Resolve existing global build and lint blockers, including the incomplete
   `/contact` route.
2. Connect the estimate form to a secure, tested submission workflow with clear
   validation, spam protection, and notifications.
3. Audit every public route for metadata, one H1, valid links, meaningful image
   alt text, and mobile CTA behavior.
4. Replace temporary or unrelated service imagery with approved, service-specific
   assets.
5. Verify all business claims, service areas, credentials, and sample content.

## Phase 2 — Local authority and conversion

1. Add accurate route-level metadata and a confirmed canonical/site URL.
2. Implement validated local-business and service structured data.
3. Build strong cross-links among related services and the service overview.
4. Add approved, verified reviews and project proof.
5. Establish privacy-conscious measurement for calls, form submissions, and CTA
   performance.

## Phase 3 — Content depth

1. Expand active service pages with distinctive, helpful content where gaps
   remain.
2. Publish useful supporting resources only when the business can maintain them:
   storm-preparation guidance, tree-care timing, and estimate preparation.
3. Consider individual location pages only when each offers meaningful local
   value beyond a rewritten city list.

## Phase 4 — Optimization

1. Review search-query and conversion data to improve high-intent landing pages.
2. Test CTA wording, form friction, content order, and service-card paths with
   measured hypotheses.
3. Conduct recurring performance, accessibility, metadata, and broken-link
   audits.
4. Refresh imagery, reviews, service claims, and documentation as the business
   evolves.

## Definition of done for roadmap work

A roadmap item is complete only when its business facts are verified, its code
and content satisfy `AGENTS.md`, its user-facing path has been tested,
and its measurement or maintenance owner is known.
